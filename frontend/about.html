About Fraudette AI

Building intelligent fraud detection systems for Indonesia’s evolving digital payment ecosystem.

MISSION

Why We Built Fraudette AI

Digital transactions in Indonesia are accelerating at an unprecedented pace. As financial ecosystems expand, fraud tactics evolve just as quickly, becoming more complex, automated, and harder to detect.

Fraudette AI exists to restore trust in digital payments. We combine advanced machine learning with explainable risk modeling to deliver real-time fraud detection that is transparent, scalable, and reliable. By analyzing behavioral patterns, transaction anomalies, and contextual signals, our system generates a clear fraud risk score from 0–100 — empowering payment providers to act instantly and confidently.

VISION

Our Vision

To become a trusted AI-powered fraud detection layer for Southeast Asia’s digital finance ecosystem.

We believe fraud prevention should be fast, adaptive, and explainable — not a black box. Fraudette AI prioritizes clarity in risk scoring so financial teams can understand why a transaction is flagged.

TECHNOLOGY

Technology Stack

Fraudette AI structures transaction data into JSON format and processes it using cloud-based AI services for contextual fraud reasoning.

Cloud Infrastructure

PROJECT

Built for Paylabs Mini Hackathon 2026

Fraudette AI was developed as part of the Paylabs Mini Hackathon 2026 challenge, focusing on secure digital payment innovation.

The goal of this project is to demonstrate how AI can enhance payment gateway security, reduce fraud losses, and improve trust in Indonesia’s fintech ecosystem.
